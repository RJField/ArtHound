Design Notes

That's the DCC integration piece from your original three pillars coming back around. And in this context it makes complete sense — the review submission is the natural trigger point for that integration, not some abstract spec payload.

Artist hits a button in Maya, screenshot captures, review ticket created, linked to the asset, lands in the lead's morning dashboard. Zero context switching, zero manual entry. That's the workflow that actually gets adopted because it removes friction at the point where friction currently kills compliance.

The engine capture for games assets is particularly strong — a video of the asset in-engine as the review artifact is far more useful than a render, and no current tool makes that easy to submit.

This also means your buyer conversation just got more interesting. You're not just selling to the production lead, you're selling something artists will actually want to use. Bottom-up adoption plus top-down value prop is a much easier sale than tools that only serve one side.

The DCC button is probably a later build given the SDK work involved, but the review submission flow itself — even as a simple web form with file upload — is achievable soon and gets you the workflow. The Maya/engine integration is the polish layer on top of a process that already works.

Positioning

Art Hound is the world's first Asset-Centric Intelligence platform. We replace fragmented task-tracking with a unified Asset Payload—integrating matrix-driven estimations, automated metadata audits, and real-time investment tracking into a single, scalable output.

Intelligence Layer

1. The Contextual Pivot (LoreBot)

Standard tools treat every task the same. LoreBot allows you to justify "inefficiency." If a specific asset is taking 3x longer than the matrix suggested, LoreBot can flag that it's because it's a "Legendary Tier" item with complex brand requirements. It turns a "red flag" into an "informed choice."

2. The Predictive Guardrail (NumbersBot)

Because it looks at tasking changes (extensions) in real-time, NumbersBot isn't just a reporter; it's a Forecaster. It can warn a producer: "Based on current status extensions, you will exhaust the Investment payload for this milestone 4 days early."

3. The Friction Remover (ProcessBot)

This is where you solve the human element. If an asset has been "In Review" for 48 hours but the "Development" was only 4 hours, ProcessBot exposes the invisible cost of waiting.

Strategic Positioning: "The Narrative Ledger"

You are essentially building a Narrative Ledger for every asset.

  • The Ledger: Art Hound Schema
  • The Audit: NumbersBot + ProcessBot
  • The Justification: LoreBot
